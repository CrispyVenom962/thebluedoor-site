Founder · William Ayers

International experience.
Personal attention.

A graduate of Baruch College in New York City, William Ayers brings experience across residential real estate, commercial asset management and entrepreneurship.

He began his career in New York’s luxury residential market, working in sales and leasing for prominent developers. In Canada, he moved into commercial and industrial asset management, with responsibilities spanning budgets, leasing and property operations.

William subsequently founded and grew a retail and service business across multiple Canadian locations, building teams and overseeing customer experience and day-to-day operations.

That combination of property knowledge and hands-on business leadership shapes The Blue Door’s approach: attentive service, considered decisions and clear accountability.

The Blue Door approach

William leads the client relationship and coordinates the independent professionals and service providers suited to each engagement. You have a clear point of contact, with specialist expertise brought in where it is needed.

Our own relocation to Portugal adds a personal understanding of the questions that come with a move and the value of having a dependable contact for a home here. Property owners can engage Blue Door independently, whether or not they use our relocation services.

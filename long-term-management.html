Professional support

The right person for each responsibility.

Tenant sourcing and lease matters

Tenant sourcing, viewings and negotiations are coordinated through an independent appropriately licensed agent. Lease advice, disputes and formal recovery are handled by qualified advisers.

Payments and deposits

The starting arrangement is direct payment of rent to the owner. We agree how receipt is evidenced, who holds any deposit and which expense approvals are needed. Blue Door’s fee is invoiced separately.

Inspections and urgent attendance

Visits require agreed access and are scheduled in your proposal. Reporting an issue does not automatically include a site visit; attendance and urgent arrangements are confirmed in advance.

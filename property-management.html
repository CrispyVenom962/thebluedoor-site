Money & approvals

You keep control.
We coordinate the details.

Our standard starting arrangement has rent paid directly to your bank account and Blue Door’s management fee invoiced separately. We monitor payments using the records agreed with you.

Before work begins, we agree who may approve expenditure, who pays suppliers and how urgent issues are escalated. We do not ask for your personal banking password.

  • Repairs, materials, cleaning and other supplier charges are separate from management unless expressly included.
  • Routine coordination, physical attendance and larger project supervision are distinguished in your proposal.
  • We seek suitable solutions and competitive pricing, weighing quality, availability and reliability.
  • Any relevant supplier markup or referral payment is disclosed before agreement.

What we can coordinate

The everyday work behind a well-kept property.

Tenant communication & rent monitoring

Routine tenant questions, records of rent due and confirmed payments, reminders and escalation to you. Formal disputes and recovery action belong with your qualified legal adviser.

Cleaning, repairs & contractor access

Define the task, identify suitable providers, arrange agreed access and record completion. Coordination can be part of management; contractor labour, materials and extra attendance are separately identified.

Bills, notices & property administration

Organise agreed utility, condominium and property notices; prepare payment information and coordinate with you or your accountant. You remain responsible for funding expenses, with professional tax advice handled separately.

Home checks, keys & arrivals

Agree a visit schedule, lawful access, visual observations, photo updates where appropriate and preparations for your arrival. Key custody and authorised access are confirmed before service begins.

Changing managers or starting with an existing tenant

You do not need to have bought or relocated through us. We can assess an existing property, identify records and keys required and coordinate a handover with your authorisation.

Your questions

Before you put us in charge.

How much does management cost?

We prepare a proposal for the property and workload. It identifies the management fee, applicable taxes, included visits and administration, and separately charged work before you commit. Choose a service plan above, then receive a property-specific quote with a recurring fee and a defined allowance.

Are repairs and visits included?

Repairs and supplier invoices are additional unless expressly included. Scheduled visits and any extra attendance are specified in your proposal, so the same included visit is not charged again.

Can you find a tenant or help with a purchase?

We can introduce an independent appropriately licensed real-estate agent. Tenant sourcing, viewings, negotiations and other mediation are handled through that agent; our management service has a separate scope.

What happens when the property is vacant or rent is late?

Your proposal explains reminders, escalation, no-rent periods and any separately selected vacant-home care. Formal legal action is handled by your adviser.

Do you provide 24-hour attendance?

There is no default promise of 24-hour attendance. Contact hours, urgent arrangements, local availability and any out-of-hours charges are agreed for your property.

How do I end the service?

Notice, outstanding fees and handover are defined in your agreement. The handover covers keys and the agreed property records for you or your authorised replacement.

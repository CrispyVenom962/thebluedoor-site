01 / MANAGEMENT

Full Property Management

10% of monthly rent, plus VAT

For an existing long-term rental that needs routine local coordination.

  • Tenant contact and rent monitoring

    We are the tenant’s first point of contact for questions and problems, so you are not. We keep a monthly record of the rent received and tell you promptly if a payment is late.

  • Repairs and maintenance, with your approval

    When something needs fixing or servicing, such as a leaking tap, a boiler check or repainting, we arrange the provider and get a quote. Nothing goes beyond the limit you set without your approval, and we keep a record of what was approved and done.

  • Owner reports and property visits

    A regular update on visits, repairs, invoices and anything waiting for your decision. We also visit the property when needed, for example to let in a tradesperson or look at a problem, with the number of visits agreed in advance.

02 / SECOND HOME

Second Home / Owner Away

Fixed monthly fee, plus VAT

For a home you use occasionally and want prepared for your return.

  • Regular checks while you’re away

    We visit at the intervals you agree and look for visible problems such as leaks, damp, pests or signs of a break-in, then report back to you.

  • Preparing for your arrival and departure On request

    An optional extra, quoted separately for your property. Before you arrive we can arrange for the home to be aired and cleaned, with the heating or air conditioning on and groceries in. When you leave, we coordinate the clean and make sure the home is locked up the way you want.

  • Cleaning, deliveries and upkeep

    We arrange cleaners, receive agreed deliveries and book routine upkeep with independent providers. Costs are separate and you approve them first.

03 / OVERSIGHT

Essential Property Care

Fixed monthly fee, plus VAT

For owners who need scheduled checks and a clear record of their home’s condition.

  • Scheduled property checks

    A set number of visits, agreed in advance, in which we walk through the property and look for visible problems. Extra visits can be agreed whenever you need them.

  • Photo updates and an action list

    After each check you receive photos and a short list of anything that needs your attention, such as a stain on a ceiling or a broken shutter, so you can decide what to do.

  • Key holding and controlled access

    We keep a set of keys safely and only let in people you have authorised, such as a cleaner or a repair provider, and we keep a record of who entered and when.

What we can coordinate

The everyday work behind a well-kept property.

Tenant communication & rent monitoring

Routine tenant questions, records of rent due and confirmed payments, reminders and escalation to you. Formal disputes and recovery action belong with your qualified legal adviser.

Cleaning, repairs & contractor access

Define the task, identify suitable providers, arrange agreed access and record completion. Coordination can be part of management; contractor labour, materials and extra attendance are separately identified.

Bills, notices & property administration

Organise agreed utility, condominium and property notices; prepare payment information and coordinate with you or your accountant. You remain responsible for funding expenses, with professional tax advice handled separately.

Home checks, keys & arrivals

Agree a visit schedule, lawful access, visual observations, photo updates where appropriate and preparations for your arrival. Key custody and authorised access are confirmed before service begins.

Changing managers or starting with an existing tenant

You do not need to have bought through us. We can assess an existing property, identify records and keys required and coordinate a handover with your authorisation.

How to become a client

From first conversation to signed agreement.

Tell us about your property

Share the location, current use and what you would like handled. A short conversation by WhatsApp, email or call is enough.

We review and propose

We confirm we can cover the property properly, then send a written proposal with the scope, fee, VAT and notice period.

Sign the agreement

You sign a property management agreement. It sets out the services, approval limits, reporting and your authorisation for us to act on the agreed matters.

Handover and start

We take over keys, tenant contact details and records, agree how and when we report to you, and begin.

What we will ask for

Owner ID and NIF, the property’s registration papers (caderneta predial and certidão permanente), the tenancy contract if there is one, the energy certificate, condominium details, any insurance policy and a set of keys. We suggest your own legal adviser reviews the agreement before you sign.

Your questions

Before you put us in charge.

How much does management cost?

Managing a rented property costs 10% of the monthly rent, plus VAT. Second-home and oversight plans have a fixed monthly fee, plus VAT, quoted for your property. Your written proposal confirms the fee and exactly what it covers before you commit. Repairs, supplier invoices and a licensed agent’s tenant-finding fee are separate, and you approve them first.

What do I sign, and when?

A written property management agreement, after you have reviewed our proposal. It sets out the services, fee, approval limits, how rent and expenses are handled, reporting, the notice period and the handover, and it authorises us to act for you on the agreed matters. We suggest your own legal adviser reviews it first.

Are repairs and visits included?

Repairs and supplier invoices are separate and follow the approval limits you set. Scheduled visits are listed in your agreement, and any extra attendance is agreed with you beforehand.

Can you find a tenant?

Yes, through an independent, appropriately licensed real-estate agent. Tenant sourcing, viewings and negotiation are handled by that agent, whose fee is separate and agreed with you before we proceed. We coordinate the process and look after the tenancy once it begins.

Do you collect the rent?

Under our standard arrangement the tenant pays rent directly into your bank account. We monitor payments using the records agreed with you and tell you promptly if one is late. We never ask for your online banking passwords.

What happens if rent is late or the property is vacant?

We follow up with the tenant, keep a record and tell you promptly. If it continues, formal action is referred to your legal adviser. Your agreement also covers how a vacant period is handled, including any separately chosen vacant-home care.

Do I need to live in Portugal?

No. The service is designed for owners who live abroad or elsewhere in Portugal, as well as those nearby. You receive regular reports and decide the matters that need you.

Are you an estate agent? Do you manage holiday lets?

No to both. We are a property manager, not an estate agency, and we do not buy or sell property. We manage long-term residential lettings and second homes, and we do not run short-stay or holiday rentals.

Do you provide 24-hour attendance?

No. We are not a 24-hour emergency service. Contact hours and how urgent issues are handled are agreed for your property and set out in your agreement.

How do I end the service?

There is no lock-in. Either side can end the agreement with 30 days’ written notice. Fees run to the end of the notice period, and we hand over the keys, tenant contact details and the agreed property records to you or your authorised replacement.

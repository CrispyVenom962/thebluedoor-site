Privacy & service information

Clear expectations
from the first conversation.

This page explains the website enquiry process and the basis on which services are discussed. Your written proposal and agreement set out the specific work.

Enquiries and personal information

The enquiry form prepares a message in your browser. It does not submit the information to a Blue Door server. You can review the message before choosing to open WhatsApp and send it there, or copy it for another contact route. Form entries are not saved by this website.

Please provide only the details needed to discuss your enquiry. Do not include passports, bank credentials, tenant documents or sensitive financial information. Necessary property details and documents can be requested through an agreed process later.

WhatsApp and the external consultation booking service process information under their own privacy terms when you use them. This website also requests fonts from Google Fonts; that provider receives the connection information needed to serve those files.

For questions about information you have provided, contact Blue Door through the contact page. Before onboarding, confirm the contracting entity and the privacy information applicable to the services you select.

Service scope and professional boundaries

Website information is general and is not a service agreement or a guarantee of a particular outcome. Scope, availability, contact hours, attendance, fees, applicable taxes and third-party costs are agreed in writing before work begins.

Blue Door’s role is practical coordination, administration and agreed property oversight. Legal, immigration, tax, financial, insurance and real-estate mediation matters are referred to independent appropriately qualified professionals.

Property visits are scheduled visual observations of agreed accessible areas, not technical surveys or continuous monitoring. Short-stay services require separate assessment of eligibility and operating arrangements.

Expenses and suppliers

Repairs, materials, cleaning and other third-party services are separately charged unless expressly included in your proposal. Spending approvals, payment routes and any relevant supplier markup or referral payment are explained before agreement.

Coverage and response

Enquiries are welcome across Portugal. Physical coverage, travel requirements and urgent attendance arrangements are confirmed for each property. There is no general promise of immediate or 24-hour attendance.

Guides and external information

Relocation guides are background information. Procedures, requirements and availability can change. Confirm current information with the relevant authority or qualified adviser before acting.

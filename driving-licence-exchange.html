Exchanging a Foreign Driving Licence in Portugal

What changes when you become resident, and the deadline that catches people out.

Why this matters

A foreign driving licence doesn't stay valid indefinitely once you become a resident of Portugal. At some point you're required to exchange it for a Portuguese one through the IMT (Instituto da Mobilidade e dos Transportes). Miss the window and you can lose the right to a straightforward exchange — meaning theory and practical exams instead.

Visiting versus resident

A visitor can generally drive in Portugal on a valid foreign licence for a limited period from entry — this is about visiting, not residency. Once you formally establish residence, the resident exchange rules apply, regardless of how much of that visitor window is left.

The deadline varies more than people expect

How long you have to exchange your licence depends heavily on where it was issued. Some countries have a much shorter window (measured in months) than others, and a handful of countries have longer bilateral arrangements. This is exactly the kind of detail that's easy to get wrong by assuming your situation matches someone else's — the honest answer is that it depends on your specific licence and nationality.

What's typically needed

  • Your valid, non-expired foreign licence — an expired licence generally can't be exchanged directly
  • Your NIF
  • Proof of legal residence in Portugal
  • A medical certificate (issued by a doctor in Portugal)
  • For many countries: a certificate of authenticity from your home country's licensing authority, plus a certified translation if the licence isn't in English or Portuguese

The process itself

Applications are submitted through the IMT's online services portal. The exact steps and required documents can shift as the system is updated, so it's worth checking the current process before you start rather than relying on older guides — including this one.

Worth knowing: deadlines, reciprocity rules, and the exact online process all vary by country and change over time. Confirm your specific deadline and requirements with the IMT as early as possible after you become resident — don't assume you have the maximum window.

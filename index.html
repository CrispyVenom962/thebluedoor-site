WHO IT’S FOR

Owners of property in Porto

Landlords with a tenant, owners preparing to rent, and people who own a home here but live elsewhere or simply don’t want the daily work.

WHAT WE DO

We run it day to day

Tenant contact, rent monitoring, repair and maintenance coordination, owner reporting, and introductions to licensed agents when you need a tenant found.

WHAT WE DON’T DO

No sales, no holiday lets

We are not an estate agency and we don’t buy or sell property. We don’t run short-stay or holiday rentals, and we don’t offer relocation services.

Your questions

Before you put us in charge.

How much does management cost?

Managing a rented property costs 10% of the monthly rent, plus VAT. Second-home and oversight plans have a fixed monthly fee, plus VAT, quoted for your property. Your written proposal confirms the fee and exactly what it covers before you commit. Repairs, supplier invoices and a licensed agent’s tenant-finding fee are separate, and you approve them first.

What do I sign, and when?

A written property management agreement, after you have reviewed our proposal. It sets out the services, fee, approval limits, how rent and expenses are handled, reporting, the notice period and the handover. We suggest your own legal adviser reviews it first.

Do you collect the rent?

No. The tenant pays the rent directly into your bank account, so your money never passes through us. We monitor that each payment arrives, and if one is late we tell you promptly and follow up as agreed with you. We never ask for your online banking passwords.

Can you find a tenant?

Yes, through an independent, appropriately licensed real-estate agent, whose fee is separate and agreed with you before we proceed. We coordinate the process and look after the tenancy once it begins.

How do I end the service?

There is no lock-in. Either side can end the agreement with 30 days’ written notice. We hand over the keys, tenant contact details and the agreed property records.

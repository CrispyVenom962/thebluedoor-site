Before you book

Clear boundaries. No surprises.

What does appointment coordination include?

We organise practical checklists, documents, timing and follow-up with you. You provide and approve the information and attend or submit applications where required. Specialist advice, legal representation and authority decisions are outside the package.

Are accompanied appointments charged again?

Settle includes four support sessions of up to two hours each. Within Porto metro, these can be accompanied in-person visits. Outside Porto metro, support is virtual; we do not travel to appointments in the Algarve or other regions. An included session is not charged again. Extra sessions, time beyond the allowance and out-of-hours support require a separate agreed quote. Start and Move do not include physical attendance.

When does my support period start?

The period starts at your first planning call: 30 days for Start, 90 for Move and 180 for Settle. We agree that start date around your plans before booking. An authority delay does not guarantee an appointment within that period; extensions are quoted in advance.

What costs are separate?

Government and application fees, lawyers, accountants, translations, apostilles, insurance, rent and deposits, purchases, cleaning, transport, trades and other supplier invoices are separate. We coordinate the work included in your package; we do not include the supplier’s bill.

Can I upgrade or add a particular service?

Yes. We identify remaining work and confirm an upgrade fee before changing your scope, taking account of work already paid for. School visits, extra accompaniment, furniture setup and ongoing concierge can be added by quotation.

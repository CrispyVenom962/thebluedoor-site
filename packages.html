Three ways to work with us

Every client starts somewhere different — exploring the idea, ready to commit, or wanting the whole process handled. Our three packages follow that natural progression, each building on the one before.

Essential Package

Your first step towards life in Portugal.

Designed for individuals and couples who want expert guidance before and during the early stages of their move. We'll help you navigate the key administrative requirements, provide practical advice and ensure you begin your relocation with confidence — and help you avoid many of the common mistakes made by new residents.

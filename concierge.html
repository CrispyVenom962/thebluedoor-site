Practical help

Tell us what would make life easier.

Arrival & home preparation

Coordinate agreed cleaning, household supplies and practical preparations before you arrive. Licensed transfers can be arranged through appropriate providers, subject to availability.

Cleaning & maintenance

Arrange independent cleaners and suitable tradespeople, with the work, access and charges agreed in advance. Provider labour and materials are separate unless the proposal says otherwise.

Furniture, deliveries & installation

Help coordinate delivery windows, approved access and installation by suitable providers. Purchases, delivery charges and attendance are confirmed before commitment.

Family & school coordination

Help organise school visits, introductions and practical family setup. School availability and admission decisions remain with the schools.

Appointments & language support

Arrange practical preparation, accompaniment or language support where agreed. Certified translations and regulated professional advice are scoped separately.

Vehicle & pet setup

Coordinate introductions for vehicle services, insurance, veterinary care, grooming and other agreed household needs. Independent provider charges are additional.

Professional introductions

Connect you with independent appropriately qualified legal, tax, financial or insurance professionals. Blue Door’s practical coordination does not replace their advice.
